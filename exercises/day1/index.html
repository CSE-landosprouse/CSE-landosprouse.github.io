Giraffe Things

  • Giraffes are the tallest land animals on Earth.
  • Giraffes can run as fast as 35 miles an hour.
  • Giraffes have long necks that help them reach leaves high up in trees.
  • Giraffes are herbivores and mainly eat leaves, flowers, and fruits.
  • Giraffes have a unique pattern of spots on their skin, which helps them blend into their surroundings.
