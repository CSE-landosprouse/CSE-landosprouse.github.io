Basketball Basics

Why Basketball Is Exciting

Basketball is a fast-paced sport played by two teams of five players. The goal is to score points by shooting the basketball through the opponent's hoop. Players must work together by passing, dribbling, defending, and making smart decisions during the game.

The sport is popular because every game can change quickly. A team can score several points in only a few seconds, which makes close games exciting to watch. Basketball also helps players improve their teamwork, fitness, communication, and confidence.
